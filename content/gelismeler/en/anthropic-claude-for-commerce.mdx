Anthropic announced a toolkit for building commerce agents on top of Claude. It has two sides: a "Shopping Agent" that talks to customers and runs the purchase, and a "Merchant Agent" that takes on the business's back office.

## In brief

- **What was announced:** on September 2, "Building Commerce Agents with Claude" — not a finished app, but a blueprint and code repository for developers. The Shopping Agent does catalog search, product comparison, cart building, and customer service; the Merchant Agent does sales analytics, inventory tracking, pricing/promotion recommendations, and drafts marketing campaigns.
- **Where and how:** it runs on the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Microsoft Foundry, and Google Cloud Vertex AI; it's built with the Messages API, Agent SDK, and Claude Managed Agents (beta). Available today — an open-source repo (github.com/anthropics/commerce-agents) and live demos are up.
- **Who's on board:** launch partners include Shopify, Priceline, Mastercard, Visa, Intuit, Klaviyo, Wix, Square, Accenture, Zomato, and Fetch.
- **The numbers (shared by Anthropic):** retailers using shopping agents saw carts up to 35% larger, and shoppers were 60% more likely to complete purchases. These are the company's own figures, not independent.

## Our take

This lands right in our audience's world: the "Merchant Agent" side especially — sales analytics, inventory, pricing, drafting marketing — is exactly the work a small business most wants to offload. But be clear on two things. First, this isn't a plug-and-play app; it's a developer blueprint and SDK. A solo shop owner won't install it directly — the value will most likely reach you through platforms you already use (Shopify, Square, and Wix are launch partners, so these capabilities may start showing up inside those tools). Second, the 35% and 60% figures are Anthropic's own data; directional, not independent proof. In short, the signal is that "AI runs both the shopping conversation and the merchant's back office" is turning from concept into real infrastructure, with major commerce players on board. If you sell online, expect these capabilities to appear inside your existing platform — and watch which of your tools adopt it.
