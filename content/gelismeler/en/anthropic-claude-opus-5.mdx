Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5, its most capable model, and made it available across all platforms the same day. The notable part is that the price didn't move: it costs the same as the previous Opus 4.8, yet the company says capability is meaningfully higher.

## In brief

- **What shipped:** Claude Opus 5 (model ID `claude-opus-5`), released July 24, 2026. It's available on the Claude API, Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork; it's the new default on Claude Max and the strongest option on Claude Pro.
- **Price:** $5 input / $25 output per million tokens — identical to Opus 4.8. There's also a "Fast mode" that runs 2.5× faster at 2× the base price.
- **How much better:** By Anthropic's own measurements, it's a big step over Opus 4.8 — it doubles its score on Frontier-Bench and scores "three times the next-best model" on ARC-AGI 3. In life sciences it reports gains of 10.2 points in organic chemistry and 7.7 in protein tasks.
- **Safety:** Based on an automated behavioral audit, the company calls it its "most aligned model to date."

## Our take

The real story here isn't "a new model shipped" — that happens monthly — it's that the price held flat. A more capable model at the same budget is a direct win for anyone paying per token or on Pro/Max: your tool gets stronger without you doing anything. Practical tip: if you're on Pro or Max you've already been moved to the new model, so there's no need to manually pick the old default before starting a task. One honest caveat: these benchmark figures are the company's own tests; independent comparisons take a few weeks, so it's early to call it "best at everything." Still, adding capability without raising the price is a concrete, positive move on the user's side.
